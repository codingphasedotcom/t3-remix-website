## Comments

Comment on a task and its owner answers on the board right away.

- An agent already working on the task gets your comment in its thread.
- Otherwise the assignee is started just to reply (or the CEO, for a task with no assignee). If you asked for work, it moves the task to **To do**.
- Replies start even when the board is at its **Agents at once** limit, but not while **Auto-run agents** is off or the employee is paused.

Your comments on a task also reach every subtask below it, and they stay in the agents' instructions on every later run. Say something once on the parent task and the whole tree hears it.

Agents use comments too: progress notes, decisions, review findings, and questions for you. Because agents never message each other directly, the comments on a task are the full record of how it was done.

## Mentions

Type `@` and a job, such as `@CTO` or `@QA`, to mention an employee.

- **In a new task**, the first person you mention in the title or description becomes the assignee, unless you already picked one.
- **In a chat** in the company's project, the agent sees exactly who you mean and can hand them a task. This works from any ordinary T3 Remix thread, not just the board.

## Files

Attach files a task needs, such as a video to edit from your Downloads folder, under **Files** when you create the task or later on the task.

- In the desktop app, use **Add files** or drop them in.
- In a browser, paste the file's full path.

Nothing is uploaded. Agents work from the files where they are, on the computer running T3 Remix. Subtasks see their parent's files too.

## Linked work

A task lists its **Linked work**: its branch, plus the pull requests, previews, and documents its agents report, with each pull request's status (open, draft, merged, or closed).

Agents add links as they work. Linking the same URL again updates it. Remove a wrong link with its **×**.
